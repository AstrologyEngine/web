Explore the chart

Throwaway interaction prototype. Does one selection at a time work on the wheel and in a sidebar or mobile sheet? The wheel uses plugin artwork and layout. All chart values below are synthetic fixtures, not calculated birth charts.

Guided walkthroughs and things to try

Choose Sun, then an aspect; select a house wedge or its number; choose a sign or ASC. At crossing lines choose the exact aspect from the list offered. Try crowded glyphs using the named list. Press Tab, Enter/Space, then Escape. Resize below 800px, open details, expand, and close. Changing the scenario clears the old selection.

Proposals to review: selecting again clears; blank wheel space and Escape clear; a new target replaces; keyboard activation focuses details and closing returns focus. The mobile sheet is nonmodal, leaves the wheel interactive, starts at 40% height, expands to 75%, and scrolls internally. Angles show reference text only. No swipe or drag gesture in this demo.
